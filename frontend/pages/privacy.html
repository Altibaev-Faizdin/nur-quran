Privacy Policy & Text Sources

Last updated: November 2026 · Committed to zero tracking and transparency

🛡️ 1. Our Privacy Philosophy

Noor Al-Quran is a free, non-commercial Islamic digital service built for the Ummah. We believe sacred Quranic and religious engagement must be strictly private and free of tracking, ads, and monetization.

  • Zero Ads: No third-party ad networks or tracking pixels are loaded on any page.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone.
  • Local First: Your reading preferences, local bookmarks, tasbih counts, and preferred reciters are stored directly on your device.

💾 2. What Data We Store & Where

We store only what is required to make the application operate reliably offline and across sessions:

  • Device Local Storage (localStorage): Holds your settings (selected language, reading font size, view mode, prayer calculation method, Hijri adjustment ±2 days, last used coordinates for offline fallback, and local bookmarks). Authentication tokens are never written to localStorage.
  • IndexedDB (NoorDB): Caches Arabic Quran text, translations, and downloaded Hadith collections on your device so you can read offline.
  • Optional Account & Session Cookie: If you choose to create an account, your username, optional email, bookmarks, notification settings, and scrypt-hashed password are stored in our application database. Refresh sessions use a strictly protected HttpOnly cookie (`nq_refresh`), while short-lived access tokens stay in page memory only. You can change your password or permanently delete your account anytime from your Bookmarks page.
  • Location & Prayer Times: Coordinates are only requested when you explicitly press location actions for prayer times or Qibla direction. For prayer times, coordinates are sent on-demand to `/api/prayer-times/` (which queries `api.aladhan.com`) or cached locally in your browser; if GPS permission is denied when requested, your IP may be looked up on-demand via `ipapi.co`. Coordinates are never linked to your user account.

📖 3. Canonical Islamic Sources & Attribution

Noor Al-Quran utilizes authentic, globally recognized textual editions and certified translations:

Arabic Quran Text: Uthmani Script (المصحف العثماني برواية حفص عن عاصم), sourced via AlQuran.cloud API (`quran-uthmani`).
Russian Translation (Перевод смыслов): Эльмир Кулиев (`ru.kuliev`), standard Russian translation.
English Translation: Muhammad Asad (`en.asad`, The Message of the Qur'an).
Uzbek Translation: Шайх Муҳаммад Содиқ Муҳаммад Юсуф (`uz.sodik`).
Audio Recitations: Streaming recordings from MP3Quran.net, EveryAyah.com, and Islamic Network CDN across 10 certified Qaris.
Prayer Calculations: Astronomical algorithms following MWL, ISNA, Egyptian Authority, Umm Al-Qura, Karachi, Diyanet, and SAMRF methods, with AngleBased high-latitude adjustment when dusk/dawn angles cannot be reached.
