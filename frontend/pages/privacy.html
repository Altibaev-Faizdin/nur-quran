Privacy Policy & Text Sources

Last updated: November 2026 · Committed to zero tracking and transparency

🛡️ 1. Our Privacy Philosophy

Noor Al-Quran is a free, non-commercial Islamic digital service built for the Ummah. We believe sacred Quranic and religious engagement must be strictly private and free of tracking, ads, and monetization.

  • Zero Ads: No third-party ad networks or trackers are loaded on any page.
  • Zero Data Selling: We never sell, rent, or share personal data with anyone.
  • Local First: Your reading history, bookmarks, tasbih counts, and preferred reciters are stored directly on your own device.

💾 2. What Data We Store & Where

We store only what is required to make the application operate reliably offline and across sessions:

  • Device Local Storage (localStorage): Holds your settings (selected language, dark theme, reading font size, prayer calculation method, hijri adjustment ±2 days, audio playback speed, and local bookmarks).
  • IndexedDB (NoorDB): Caches full Arabic Quran text, translations, and hadith collections so you can read without an active internet connection.
  • Account Data (Optional): If you choose to register an account, your username, email, and cryptographically hashed password (PBKDF2/SHA256) are stored securely in our database solely to sync your bookmarks across multiple devices.
  • Geolocation: Coordinates are only requested when you tap "Detect Location" for prayer times or Qibla direction, processed locally, and never transmitted to our backend.

📖 3. Canonical Islamic Sources & Attribution (UX-08)

Noor Al-Quran utilizes authentic, globally recognized textual editions and certified translations:

Arabic Quran Text: Uthmani Script (المصحف العثماني برواية حفص عن عاصم), sourced from King Fahd Complex for the Printing of the Holy Quran (Madinah Munawwarah) and Tanzil.net API.
Russian Translation (Перевод смыслов): Эльмир Кулиев (Elmir Kuliev), internationally verified standard Russian translation with explanatory notes.
English Translation: Muhammad Asad (The Message of the Qur'an) and Abdullah Yusuf Ali, respected for scholarly fidelity and literary clarity.
Uzbek Translation (Тафсири Ҳилол): Шайх Муҳаммад Содиқ Муҳаммад Юсуф (Sheikh Muhammad Sodiq Muhammad Yusuf), authoritative modern Uzbek commentary and translation.
Audio Recitations: Authorized digital recordings provided by EveryAyah.com, MP3Quran.net, and Islamic Network CDN across 10 certified Qaris.
Prayer Calculations: Astronomical algorithms following Muslim World League (MWL), Egyptian General Authority of Survey, and Umm Al-Qura (Makkah) methods, enhanced with the 1/7th night high-latitude adaptation for regions above 48°N.
